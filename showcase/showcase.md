# Wick

The city is sleeping. The dark is not. You are its last lamplighter, carrying one small flame through a night that wants it gone. Keep walking; your tools fire themselves, and where you stand matters more than how quickly you can press a button.

The first moths are almost gentle. Then come bats, crawling things, the great Mothwing, and an owl heavy enough to blot out the light. Every creature that falls leaves something worth stepping into danger for. Gather its gems, raise your lamp's level, and choose one of three gifts: a new tool, a useful trinket, or a stronger version of something you already carry.

Six tools. Six trinkets. A hundred ways to keep the dark at arm's length. Circle yourself with lanterns, scatter burning oil, or cut a path with a slash of light. The great creatures carry chests, and a chest can transform a fully grown tool when you hold its matching trinket. Consult the Almanac, then make the night your own.

At nine minutes, the Dark itself arrives. There is no final door to find and no last wave to clear. Keep your flame alive until the clock reaches ten minutes. The city will wake. Make sure you are there to see it.
